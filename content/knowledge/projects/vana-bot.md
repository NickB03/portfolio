One of my main side projects is vana.bot — a full-stack AI chat application I built that renders interactive artifacts like React components, SVG graphics, and Mermaid diagrams live in the browser. It's built with React, TypeScript, and Vite on the frontend, uses OpenRouter for LLM access, and has a Supabase/PostgreSQL backend running on Deno. You can check it out at https://vana.bot. It is a shipped full-stack AI project that reflects how I turn AI capabilities into usable product experiences.

## How it connects
vana.bot is where I first worked out the "render the model's output as something usable" pattern that I later pushed further in [[Polymorph]]. The grounded-retrieval ideas overlap with [[AnalystAI]], and the whole thing is an exercise in hands-on [[AI Engineering]].
