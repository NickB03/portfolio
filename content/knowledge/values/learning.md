I value self-directed learning and thoughtful problem solving. I prefer to investigate first, share what I learn, and stay open to better explanations when new evidence appears. I learn by doing — building something real is how I actually understand it, which is why I keep doing hands-on [[AI Engineering]] even in leadership roles.

## Approach
I'm direct, curious, and practical. I care about clear thinking and useful work, and I like helping the people around me build their own confidence.
