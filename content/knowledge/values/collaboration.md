I value clear thinking, useful work, and collaboration across product, engineering, sales, marketing, and customer-facing teams. A lot of my impact comes from getting those groups aligned — translating between technical and business contexts so everyone is building toward the same vision.

## Balance
I try to balance ambition with being present for the people around me. Good [[Product Strategy]] is a team sport, and I'd rather move a group forward together than be right alone.
