This AI chat is part of Nick's portfolio. It uses retrieval-augmented generation to answer from selected portfolio knowledge rather than guessing, and pulls in related context through a small knowledge graph built from the notes that feed it.

## Stack
It's built with React, TypeScript, and Next.js, with Supabase and pgvector for retrieval and Google Gemini for embeddings and responses — a small showcase of Nick's own [[AI Engineering]]. If the assistant can't help, you can reach Nick at nbohmer@gmail.com or on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/nickbohmer.
